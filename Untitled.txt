
## 🎨 Icon Generator
To generate icons, you can use:
1. **Favicon.io** - Upload a 512x512 image and download all sizes
2. **RealFaviconGenerator.net** - Comprehensive PWA icon generator
3. Or use the simple icon provided in the icons folder
